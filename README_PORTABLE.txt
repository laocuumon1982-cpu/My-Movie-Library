MY MOVIE LIBRARY v3.0 by Duy Anh - BAN PORTABLE (khong can cai dat)

1. Giai nen ra bat ky dau (o D:, USB...). Chay "My Movie Library.exe".
2. Cam o HDD vao may -> bam "+ Quet o HDD" -> chon o -> "Bat dau quet".
   Tool quet toan bo thu muc, thu muc con va ten file, luu vao thu vien kem MA SO:
     o HDD: H01, H02...   thu muc: H01-D00012   file: H01-000123
3. Tim kiem tuc thi (go khong dau): ten phim | ext:mkv | >1gb | hdd:H01 | ma file/thu muc.
   Rut o ra van tim duoc - thu vien luu san trong thu muc Data\library.db.
4. Quet lai o (khi them/xoa phim): chon o -> "Quet lai". Nut "Update" tu cap nhat ban moi tu GitHub.
